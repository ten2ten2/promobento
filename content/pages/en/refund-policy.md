## 1. Refunds for unused purchases

We offer a full voluntary refund when you request it within {{policy.voluntaryRefundDays}} days after payment and **none of the allowances granted by that order has been used**. This includes video exports, full-video creation, AI revisions, and media processing.

Purchases apply to one project and are one-time payments. There is no subscription or automatic renewal to cancel. Deleting a project or closing your account does not itself request a refund.

## 2. What counts as use

We check the activity charged to the order, including activity by authorized workspace members:

- **Creation and revisions:** an allowance is used when a usable result is delivered. Choosing not to accept its appearance does not make it unused.
- **Media processing:** an allowance is used when usable processed media is delivered.
- **Exports:** an allowance is used when the confirmed initial export files for that video are successfully delivered and available for download, whether or not you download them. Preparing additional included platform formats later does not postpone that use.

A temporary reservation, a placeholder, or a failed or cancelled task that delivers no usable result does not count as use. Technical defects are handled separately under Section 3. Having no exported video does not necessarily mean your order is unused: creation, revisions, or media processing may already have used its allowances.

A single-export purchase starts delivery after payment. Once the confirmed initial files are available for download, that purchase has been used and no longer qualifies for the voluntary unused-purchase refund. Delivery problems, technical defects, and legal rights remain covered by Section 3.

## 3. Billing errors, delivery problems, and legal rights

Contact us about duplicate charges, an ongoing failure to deliver a paid service, or a technical defect. These requests are assessed separately from the voluntary unused-purchase policy, including after its {{policy.voluntaryRefundDays}}-day window. Our costs from failed attempts do not remove your delivery rights.

For a technical defect in delivered work, we will first offer a repair without an additional charge where appropriate and consistent with your legal rights. If we cannot fix it, we will assess a refund for the affected purchase or delivery.

Nothing here limits mandatory cancellation, withdrawal, refund, or other consumer rights. Using the service does not automatically waive those rights.

Where the law requires it, checkout asks separately for your request or consent to start during a withdrawal period, explains any effect on that right, and provides the required purchase confirmation. The rules can differ between delivery of a confirmed video and a pack of creation services. Agreeing to the Terms, paying, or using one allowance does not by itself waive every right for an entire pack.

## 4. How to request a refund

The workspace owner can find the purchase in [Orders]({{links.orders}}) and submit a request through [Help]({{links.help}}). You can also email [{{brand.supportEmail}}](mailto:{{brand.supportEmail}}), including if you cannot access your account.

Include your account email, order or receipt reference, and reason for requesting a refund. We may ask for information needed to verify the purchase or understand a problem. Do not send full payment-card details.

## 5. Review, upgrades, and additional packs

While we review a request, we may pause the affected unused allowances and unfinished work that depends on them. This does not cancel unrelated purchases or automatically remove rights in delivered videos.

An upgrade depends on its original purchase; an additional pack depends on the qualifying project purchase or upgrade. We cannot refund a base purchase while leaving related paid purchases without their required access.

Before proceeding, we will identify the related orders, proposed amounts, allowances that would end, and treatment of delivered videos, and ask the owner to confirm the scope. Used allowances, partial refunds, and complications involving related orders receive individual review. Partial refunds are not calculated automatically from the proportion of unused allowances.

If one related refund cannot be completed, we will pause and resolve the remaining purchases without treating them as refunded or silently removing their allowances.

## 6. Project access after a refund

A completed refund removes the allowances covered by that refund. If a refund fails, we restore paused access that remains valid, subject to the project's permissions, expiry, and deletion status. Refunds do not reset your free-trial eligibility or previous use.

We recalculate the project's expiry using its remaining valid purchases. If a refund brings automatic media deletion forward, deletion will occur no earlier than {{policy.refundNoticeDays}} days after notice and no earlier than the expiry and grace period supported by another valid purchase.

Access supported by another valid purchase continues until its normal expiry. If files are retained only for the additional notice period after normal access ends, they are read-only; downloads require a valid existing permission. Refunded creation or export allowances are not restored. A separate request to delete your project follows our [Privacy Policy]({{links.privacy}}) and does not wait for this notice period.

## 7. Rights in delivered videos

Unused-allowance refunds, partial refunds, and service compensation do not by themselves revoke commercial permissions lawfully obtained for delivered videos. Refunding an upgrade or additional pack does not revoke permissions from unrelated valid purchases.

For an exceptional full refund of an already delivered order, we will explain the affected videos and any proposed change to their permitted use. Such a change needs a valid contractual or legal basis. A refund status alone does not revoke every video license or reverse a transfer of intellectual-property rights. Cloud download access is separate from these rights.

## 8. Payment status and disputes

A request or approval is not confirmation that money has been returned. We confirm completion after Waffo verifies the refund; when it appears on your statement depends on the payment provider and bank.

During a payment dispute, we may pause related unsettled services while the outcome is reviewed. An investigation is not a final refund. Any resulting adjustment is limited to the affected transactions and applicable rights. Contacting us first may help resolve a problem, but is not a condition for exercising lawful rights with your payment provider.
